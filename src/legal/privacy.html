Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · Terms of Service

This policy explains what information CreativeFlow collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the CreativeFlow desktop app, the web and phone versions, and the CreativeFlow servers they connect to.

1. Who we are

CreativeFlow is operated by Rich Malone, Iceland ("CreativeFlow", "we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

Contact for anything privacy-related: creativeflowofficial1@gmail.com.

3. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contract — to provide the app and membership you signed up for (account, your content, sync, Adonis, membership checks).
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix bugs you report.
  • Legal obligations — for example, keeping records required by law.

4. Who we share it with

We use these service providers ("processors") to run CreativeFlow. They may only use your data to provide their service to us:

  • Clerk — sign-in and account management.
  • Netlify — hosting our servers and storing your synced content and bug reports.
  • Anthropic — the AI model behind Adonis. Messages you send to Adonis are passed to Anthropic to generate a reply.
  • Gumroad — selling memberships, handling payments and issuing license keys. Gumroad's own privacy policy applies to your purchase.
  • Discord — bug reports are delivered to a private CreativeFlow channel on Discord so we can read them.
  • Google (YouTube API Services) — when you use Analytics or Competitors, the channel links you enter are sent to YouTube to look up public channel stats. See section 11.

We may also share information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of CreativeFlow, our members or others.

5. International transfers

Several of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States and other countries outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, transfers rely on safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and synced content — for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account in the app, your synced content is deleted from our servers straight away and your sign-in account is removed.
  • Data on your device — the app also keeps a copy on your device so it works offline. Signing out or deleting your account removes it.
  • Adonis — our server doesn't keep your conversations; the chat history lives in your account. Anthropic keeps messages sent through its service only for a limited period under its own policies.
  • Bug reports — up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Server logs and usage counters — kept briefly (usually days to weeks) for security and fair use.
  • Purchase records — kept by Gumroad, and by us where accounting law requires.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export and delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. In the app you can:

  • Export your data — Account → Export backup file.
  • Correct your profile — Account → Edit Profile.
  • Delete your account and data — Account → Delete my account and data.

For anything else, email creativeflowofficial1@gmail.com. We'll reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Icelandic Data Protection Authority, Persónuvernd (personuvernd.is), or the authority in the country where you live.

8. Minimum age

CreativeFlow is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child under 16 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, your content is stored per account, and access to our systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your license key to yourself.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app (on the Updates & help page) before they take effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

11. YouTube API Services

CreativeFlow's Analytics and Competitors features use YouTube API Services to show public information about YouTube channels and their videos. By using these features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information YouTube provides is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

  • We only read public channel and video information (such as subscriber counts, views and video titles). We don't ask for access to your Google or YouTube account, and we can't see or change anything private.
  • The channel links you enter are saved in your account so the app can show them again. Stats fetched from YouTube are kept on our servers for up to an hour to avoid repeat lookups, then refreshed.
  • To stop, remove the channel in Analytics or Competitors, or delete your account. To ask us to delete anything, email creativeflowofficial1@gmail.com.
